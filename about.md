# Jordan's Crossing

### Refuge. Restoration. Resources

"See! I am doing a new thing" Isaiah 43:19

### HELPING WOMEN AND CHILDREN ESCAPE DOMESTIC VIOLENCE AND FIND HEALING IN CHRIST

## ABOUT

Jordan's Crossing was born out of a desire to walk with women and children through the darkest calleys of life. Named after the biblical crossing into the Promised Land (Joshua 3). We believe healing and new begninnings await on the other side of pain.
We stand on Isaiah 43:19: "See! I am doing a new thing! Now it springs up; do you not perceive it? I am making a way in the wilderness and streams in the wasteland."
This promis reminds us that God brings hope, even in devastation. At Jordan's Crossing we believe God is creating something beautoful from the ashes - a safe place for women and children to rebuild their lives.
## MISSION

To provide refuge, restoration, and resources to women and children escaping domestic violence, empowering mothers to rebuild safe, self-sufficient lives in a Christ-centered community

## VISION

A world where every woman and child fleeing abuse has a safe place to land, heal, and grow - free of fear and full of hope

## Core Values

- Compassion
- Dignity
- Empowerment
- Faith
- Integrity

## THREE PILLARS OF SUPOPRT

1. Refuge - Safe transitional housing in an undisclosed location
2. Restoration - Faith-based counseling and life-skills programs
3. Resources - Job readiness and financial literacy

## WHERE ARE WE NOW

Jordan's Crossing is in the beginning stages. While our mission is clear, housing development had not yet started. The need is immediate and your help is crucial
