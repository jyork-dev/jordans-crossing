About Us

Refuge. Restoration. Resources.

"See! I am doing a new thing" — Isaiah 43:19

Jordan's Crossing was born out of a desire to walk with women and children through the darkest alleys of life. Named after the biblical crossing into the Promised Land, we believe healing and new beginnings await on the other side of pain.

We stand on Isaiah 43:19 and believe God brings hope, even in devastation. Jordan's Crossing exists to be a safe place where women and children can rebuild their lives with dignity.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To provide refuge, restoration, and resources to women and children escaping domestic violence, empowering mothers to rebuild safe, self-sufficient lives in a Christ-centered community.

Vision

A world where every woman and child fleeing abuse has a safe place to land, heal, and grow — free of fear and full of hope.

Core Values

  • Compassion
  • Dignity
  • Empowerment
  • Faith
  • Integrity

Three Pillars of Support

  1. Refuge — Safe transitional housing in an undisclosed location.
  2. Restoration — Faith-based counseling and life-skills programs.
  3. Resources — Job readiness and financial literacy.

Where We Are Now

Jordan's Crossing is in the beginning stages. While our mission is clear, housing development has not yet started. The need is immediate and your help is crucial.

Programs

Emergency Support

Immediate safety planning and short-term housing placements for women and their children.

Transitional Housing

Longer-term housing with case management while clients secure independent living.

Employment & Training

Job-readiness workshops, resume support, and connections to employers.
