About us

Jordan's Crossing is a non-profit dedicated to helping women safely leave abusive relationships and rebuild their lives through immediate support, transitional housing, counseling, and job-readiness programs. We focus on safety, dignity, and long-term stability.

Programs

Emergency support

Immediate safety planning and short-term housing placements for women and their children.

Transitional housing

Longer-term housing with case management while clients secure independent living.

Employment & training

Job-readiness workshops, resume support, and connections to employers.
